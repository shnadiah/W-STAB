# W-STAB
W-AF Risk Score: INR Stabilization Stratification Tool
An interactive, web-based clinical decision support hub designed to assist healthcare professionals (doctors and pharmacists) in predicting the likelihood of INR stabilization in patients at 6-month and 12-month intervals.

📌 Project Overview
The W-AF Risk Score tool implements the W-STAB predictive models into a user-friendly, mobile-responsive interface. This tool categorizes patients into "Likely" or "Less Likely" to achieve a stable International Normalized Ratio (INR) based on specific clinical variables and patient demographics.

🛠 Features
W-STAB 6 Score: Predicts INR stabilization within the first 6 months of therapy.

W-STAB 12 Score: Predicts INR stabilization within the first 12 months of therapy.

Responsive Design: Fully optimized for desktops, tablets, and smartphones.

Real-time Calculation: Instant score updates as clinical variables are toggled.

Professional UI: Designed with a clean, high-contrast "Clinical Portal" aesthetic.

🧬 Clinical Models Included
W-STAB 6
Calculated based on variables including:

Ethnicity

Serum albumin levels

Presence of Valvular Heart Disease

DM with microvascular complications

Hyperthyroidism status

Timing of first INR assessment and its initial value

W-STAB 12
Calculated based on variables including:

Age category

Alcohol consumption

Heart failure status

eGFR (Renal function)

Total weekly warfarin dose and days to achieve the first therapeutic INR
